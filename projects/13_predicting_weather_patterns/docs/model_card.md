# Model Card: Predicting Weather Patterns (ML013)

## Model Details
- **Developer:** MLFORGE Team
- **Model Date:** September 2026
- **Model Version:** 1.0.0
- **Model Type:** Multivariate Weather Regressor (LightGBM/RandomForest)
- **License:** MIT Research License

## Intended Use
- **Primary Intended Uses:** Research, benchmarking, educational exploration, and decision-support simulation.
- **Out of Scope Uses:** Research forecasting module. For life-safety severe storm alerts, rely on national meteorological authorities.

## Factors, Metrics & Evaluation Data
- **Target Metrics:** mae, rmse, r2_score
- **Dataset:** `meteorological_obs_sample` with stratified splits and cross-validation.

## Ethical Considerations & Responsible AI
Research forecasting module. For life-safety severe storm alerts, rely on national meteorological authorities.

## Caveats and Recommendations
Atmospheric chaos limits deterministic long-range forecasts past short-term horizons.
