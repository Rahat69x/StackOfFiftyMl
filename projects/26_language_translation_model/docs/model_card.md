# Model Card: Language Translation Model (ML026)

## Model Details
- **Developer:** MLFORGE Team
- **Model Date:** September 2026
- **Model Version:** 1.0.0
- **Model Type:** Seq2Seq Encoder-Decoder with Bahdanau Attention Baseline
- **License:** MIT Research License

## Intended Use
- **Primary Intended Uses:** Research, benchmarking, educational exploration, and decision-support simulation.
- **Out of Scope Uses:** Not certified for official legal, diplomatic, or medical document translation without professional certified human translators.

## Factors, Metrics & Evaluation Data
- **Target Metrics:** bleu_score, chrf_score, exact_match_ratio
- **Dataset:** `bilingual_sentences_sample` with stratified splits and cross-validation.

## Ethical Considerations & Responsible AI
Not certified for official legal, diplomatic, or medical document translation without professional certified human translators.

## Caveats and Recommendations
Prone to hallucinations or literal mistranslations of cultural idioms and legal terminology.
